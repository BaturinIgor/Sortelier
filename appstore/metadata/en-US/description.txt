Sortelier is a cozy sorting puzzle about food. Drag products between kitchen shelves, cook dishes by recipe and serve the customers waiting at the counter.

COOK, DON'T JUST SORT
Three of the same product go to a customer, but three different ingredients become a dish: bun, patty and lettuce make a burger, rice, fish and soy make sushi. Flour turns into dough, dough goes into pizza. Every move counts.

FIVE KITCHENS OF THE WORLD
American diner, Japanese kitchen, Italian trattoria, Mexican cantina and French bakery: 150 hand-checked levels, each with its own products, music and background.

NEW TWISTS ALONG THE WAY
Frozen products, mystery items, picky Gourmets with big tips, shelves locked with a key product and shelves chained together.

NO TIMER, NO STRESS
Customers never leave. Serve them quickly and they tip more, take your time and they simply stop tipping.

EVERY DAY THERE IS SOMETHING
Daily and weekly challenges, a monthly medal, a streak with rewards, chef races and seasonal events with their own map, treats and costumed guests.

COLLECT IT ALL
Fill the cookbook, complete the card album, earn achievements and decorate your kitchen with new backgrounds.

No forced ads between levels. Rewarded ads are always optional.
